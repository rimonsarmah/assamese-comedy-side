01ঘৰ ক'ত?

Ghor kot?

Two Assamese people meet. First question is never “how are you”. It is “Ghor kot?”ঘৰ ক'ত?“Where is your home?” Meaning: which district, which village, whose son. Within three minutes, a common relative is found. Every time. Try it.

Tumar ghor kot? Pick one

Living in Chennai does not count. Your ghor is where Maa is.

02ৰিমনৰ দিনলিপি

Rimon's dinlipi

DinlipiদিনলিপিDiary means diary. This is one ordinary day at home in Golaghat, on leave from Chennai. Every Assamese person has lived exactly this day about four thousand times.

  1. 05:30

    dawn

    Maa's alarm

    “Uth! Beli uthil!”উঠ! বেলি উঠিল!“Get up! The sun is up!” The sun in Assam rises before the alarm does. Rimon replies “Aru pas minute.”আৰু পাঁচ মিনিট।“Five more minutes.” Both know this is a lie.

  2. 06:15

    dawn

    Lal sah, cup one

    Black tea, in a glass, on the verandah. Doesn’t count towards the daily total. Nothing before 7am counts.

  3. 08:30

    day

    Jolpan

    Chira, doi, gurচিৰা, দৈ, গুড়Flattened rice, curd, jaggery. Breakfast that is basically dessert, but cultural, so it’s healthy.

  4. 10:00

    day

    Friend calls

    “Ki kori aso?”কি কৰি আছ?“What are you doing?” — “Eneye.”এনেয়ে।“Nothing. Just like that.” — “Ahibi ne?” — “Ahi asuআহি আছোঁI'm coming.” Location: bed. Estimated arrival: 4pm.

  5. 13:00

    day

    Bhat

    Rice, masor tenga, khar, pitika, xaak, bhaji. Maa calls this “nothing special, just simple”. Five items is “simple”. One item is “starving”.

  6. 14:00

    day

    The post-rice nap

    Bhat khai tuponiভাত খাই টোপনিSleeping after eating rice. Medically required. The nation shuts down. Even the dogs are asleep.

  7. 16:30

    dusk

    Sunset (yes, already)

    Sun sets at 4:45 in winter. Tea gardens even ran their own clock an hour ahead, called Bagan timeবাগান টাইমTea-garden time, one hour ahead of IST. Rest of India still having lunch. Assam already lighting lamps.

  8. 17:00

    dusk

    Guest arrives, unannounced

    “Ahok, bohok!”আহক, বহক!“Please come, please sit!” Out come the good cups, the Britannia tin of pitha, and the tamul-paan. The guest says “just five minutes”. Leaves at 9.

  9. 18:30

    night

    Power cut

    “Current gol!”কাৰেণ্ট গ'ল!“The power's gone!” shouted by every house on the lane at the same time, as if the others didn’t notice.

  10. 19:30

    night

    Prime time

    Deuta watches the Assamese news debate at full volume. Six people shouting. Deuta shouts back. That’s seven.

  11. 23:30

    night

    Maa's final notice

    “Kiman bajat xubi? Mobile-tu thoi de!”কিমান বজাত শুবি? মোবাইলটো থৈ দে!“What time will you sleep? Put the phone down!” Rimon puts the phone down. Picks it up again. Reels until 2.

04তিনিটা বিহু

Three Bihus. One mood.

Assamese people have three Bihus a year. Outsiders know one. We correct them every time, kindly, while dancing.

The loud one

Rongali Bihu

  • 01Assamese New Year. Dhol, pepa, gogona and toka come out. The whole state becomes a dance floor for a week.
  • 02Day one is Goru Bihuগৰু বিহুCattle Bihu, the first day. Cows get a bath with turmeric and are slapped gently with gourd and brinjal while everyone sings “Lau kha, bengena kha, bosore bosore barhi ja”লাও খা, বেঙেনা খা, বছৰে বছৰে বাঢ়ি যা“Eat gourd, eat brinjal, grow bigger every year.”. Nobody has asked the cow.
  • 03You get a new gamosa as BihuwanবিহুৱানThe Bihu gift, usually a gamosa. You now own 47 gamosas. Each one is sacred. None can be thrown away.
  • 04Even Deuta dances. Deuta has not moved this fast since 1998. The video goes to every family WhatsApp group by 9pm.
